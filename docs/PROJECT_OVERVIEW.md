# JanSamadhan – Project Overview

## What is JanSamadhan?

JanSamadhan (Hindi: *"People's Solution"*) is an open, AI-powered civic platform designed to modernise the way Indian citizens interact with their local government.

## Problem Statement

Indian cities face chronic civic problems — potholes, broken streetlights, water leaks, garbage overflow — that go unresolved because:
1. Citizens lack an easy, unified channel to report issues
2. Government departments lack visibility and prioritisation tools
3. There is no transparent feedback loop for citizens tracking their complaints

## Solution

JanSamadhan provides:
- A **citizen-facing** mobile-friendly web app to report, track, and discuss civic issues
- An **AI engine** (Grok) that automatically categorises, prioritises, and routes reports
- An **official dashboard** for government departments to manage and resolve cases
- A **community collaboration** layer that lets neighbours support each other

## Target Users

| User Type | Role |
|-----------|------|
| Citizens | Report problems, track status, collaborate |
| City Officials | Review, assign, and resolve reports |
| Administrators | Manage departments, analyse data, configure platform |

## Impact Goals

- Reduce average civic issue resolution time by 40%
- Achieve 80% citizen satisfaction score within 12 months of launch
- Cover 10 Indian cities in the first year
